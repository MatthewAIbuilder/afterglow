# The totem: Afterglow on a festival tablet

The totem turns a tablet into a glowing Afterglow screen for a festival. It shows the logo and name full screen. Every few minutes a sticker pops up with a big code that anyone can scan with their phone's camera to keep. Only the support account (HQ) can run it.

## Before the festival

1. **Make the stickers.** On a computer, open Afterglow HQ → **Stickers**.
   - The quickest way is the **festival places kit**. Type a hunt name, like "Lost Lands 2027". Add an end date if you want one, then press **Make the festival places kit**.
   - That makes one sticker for each place: the Portal potty, Refill (water), Charger, Care tent, Food truck, Silent disco, Shuttle, Hammock, Main stage, Campfire and Tent.
   - You can also make single stickers yourself.
2. **Print them** (optional, for the real stickers you put up around the grounds). Press **Print all** next to the hunt and choose **Save as PDF** for a sticker printer.
   - Each one says what to do there, like "Scan to open the portal" on the porta potty ones.
3. **Set up the tablet.** Sign in to Afterglow as the support account on the tablet: in the app, or on the website at afterglowmusic.app.
   1. Open HQ and tap **Totem** at the top.
   2. Choose whose logo it shows: Afterglow, or an organizer.
   3. Type a line under the name, like "Main stage · Saturday".
   4. Pick the stickers it pops up. Tap **All of <your hunt>** to use the whole kit in one go.
   5. Choose how often a sticker pops up (every 1 to 15 minutes) and how long each stays up. A pop-up never lasts more than half the gap, so the logo always gets its time.
   - These settings stay on that tablet.

## At the festival

1. Plug the tablet in. The totem keeps the screen on while it runs, so it uses battery.
2. Tap **Start the totem**.
3. On the website, tap the screen once and choose **Full screen**.
4. To stop people leaving the app on an iPad, turn on **Guided Access**:
   1. Go to Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access and turn it on.
   2. Open the totem, then triple-click the top (or home) button and tap **Start**.
   3. To finish, triple-click again and enter your code.
5. **Tap the screen once** to show the controls for 6 seconds:
   - **Pop one now** brings the next sticker up straight away.
   - **Full screen** (website only).
   - **Stop the totem** goes back to the setup.
   - **HQ** goes back to Afterglow HQ.

## What people see

- **Between pop-ups**:
  - The logo, the name, your line, "Next one in 3:08" with a grey shape of what's coming, and how many have been claimed since the totem started.
  - A small code in the corner that opens Afterglow.
- **During a pop-up**:
  - "FREE COLLECTIBLE · SCAN TO CLAIM", the sticker's creature moving, its name and a big code.
  - The countdown, and "Claimed by N people". When someone scans it, "Just claimed!" appears with confetti.
- **The Portal potty** comes through a portal that opens across the whole screen.
- **Anyone who scans a sticker off the totem** also finds a secret, *Totem catch*.
